# Demo guide for judges

Address: https://brainwave-blitz-v6q8.onrender.com

Open the address 5 minutes before the demo. The free service sleeps when idle and the first visit can take up to a minute.

## Two minute demo

1. On a laptop, enter a name and click Create a room.
2. Show the room code. Ask someone to open the address on a phone, enter a name and the code, and tap Join. Or tap Invite friends to share a link.
3. The phone player taps Ready. The host chooses 5 questions and clicks Start match.
4. Play. Point out the wave timer, the shapes on the answers, and that faster answers score more.
5. On the final screen, show both devices with the same standings and the per question strip.
6. Click Play again to show a rematch.

## Optional: team and region demo

1. In the lobby, the host picks Team A vs Team B. Players are split into two colored columns and can switch teams.
2. Pick the Nigeria category to show the region questions.
3. After the match the results show which team won, with the team score as the average of its players.

## What to point out

- No accounts and no install.
- The server decides every score and deadline, so a modified browser cannot cheat.
- Close the phone's browser tab mid match and reopen the address: the player returns to the same seat.
- It runs on free hosting at a cost of $0.
